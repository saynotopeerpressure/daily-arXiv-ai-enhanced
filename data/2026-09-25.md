### [1] [Clinical Knowledge Graphs for Chest X-Ray Device Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29536)
*Harshil Lodhiya*

Main category: cs.AI

TL;DR: 提出一种不确定性感知的临床知识图谱，用于整合胸部X光片中的设备实例、尖端估计及报告信息，为基于证据的AI推理提供可复现的基础。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有图像模型多返回标签或分割结果，而报告处理系统虽能结构化文本却无法访问图像几何信息，缺乏两者结合的完整证据链表示。

Method: 构建包含设备实例、尖端估计、放置评估等连接证据的不确定性感知的临床知识图谱；利用RANZCR CLiP测试档案（30,083项研究）评估视觉图层，实现91万余个证据节点和88万余条关系，定义类型数据契约、不确定性表示及纵向查询机制。

Result: 构建了庞大的知识图谱材料，所有预测设备节点均保留尖端协方差、放置概率等详细字段；分析证实该方法提供了可复现的证据保存基础，但未证明其临床效用或长期性能。

Conclusion: 该工作证明了在胸部X射线设备评估中进行基于证据的AI推理的可复现基础，但当前仅为事后描述性分析，需进一步验证报告接地和临床实用性。

Abstract: Chest radiographs are routinely used to verify the position of catheters, tubes, and other support devices. Existing image models often return labels or segmentations, while report-processing systems structure text without access to image geometry. We present an uncertainty-aware clinical knowledge graph that represents device instances, tip estimates, placement assessments, provenance, report events, and temporal links as separate but connected evidence.
  We evaluate the implemented visual graph layer using saved predictions from the complete RANZCR CLiP test archive, comprising 30,083 studies from 3,255 patients across five non-overlapping outer folds. The graph builder materializes 914,632 B7 evidence nodes and 884,549 typed relationships. All 118,647 B7 predicted-device nodes retain tip covariance, placement probabilities, fragment provenance, and fragment counts, whereas the direct B2 baseline retains none of these fields. We further define typed data contracts, uncertainty representations, abstention rules, report-image grounding, and longitudinal query mechanisms for extending the graph to report-bearing cohorts.
  The reported graph-materialization analysis is post-hoc descriptive and does not establish report grounding, longitudinal performance, or clinical utility. It demonstrates a reproducible foundation for evidence-preserving AI reasoning over chest X-ray device assessments.

### [2] [AD-WM: Action-Discriminative World Models for Counterfactual Model Predictive Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30264)
*Jiabin Qiu,Zixuan Chen,Hongye Cao,Jieqi Shi,Jing Huo,Yang Gao*

Main category: cs.AI

TL;DR: 提出AD-WM，一种用于反事实模型预测控制（MPC）的行动判别性联合嵌入世界模型。通过解决事实预测误差低但行动区分能力差的问题，显著提升了仿真环境和真实机器人任务中的成功率及零样本迁移能力。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统的潜在世界模型通常训练用于预测事实性转换，但模型预测控制（MPC）需要比较来自同一状态的不同替代行动。现有模型可能存在事实预测误差低但难以区分候选行动的问题，导致规划效果不佳。

Method: 引入AD-WM，结合残差潜在动力学、预测器级的行动恢复正则化。利用逆动力学和基于条件互信息归一化的恢复目标来保留行动信息。在测试时丢弃辅助头，保持MPC不变。

Result: 在OGBench-Cube上，硬启动成功率从3.7%提升至52.0%；在四个/五个模拟环境中提高平均成功率。CEM对齐的精英后悔值更紧密地跟踪成功排序。在Franka设置中，零样本迁移的基本拾放成功率从42.2%提升至71.1%，无需实验室特定适应。

Conclusion: 用于规划的 world models 应保留反事实选择所需的行动依赖差异，而不仅仅是优化事实预测准确性。

Abstract: Latent world models are typically trained to predict factual transitions, whereas model predictive control (MPC) must compare alternative actions from the same state. A model can therefore achieve low factual prediction error yet poorly distinguish candidate actions. We introduce AD-WM, an action-discriminative joint-embedding world model for counterfactual MPC. AD-WM combines residual latent dynamics with predictor-level action-recovery regularization, using inverse dynamics and a normalized recovery objective motivated by conditional mutual information. Both objectives encourage planning transitions to preserve action information; their auxiliary heads are discarded at test time, leaving MPC unchanged. On OGBench-Cube, AD-WM improves hard-start success from 3.7% to 52.0% over a matched LeWM baseline and improves mean success over the reproduced baseline in four of five simulation environments. Planning diagnostics show that factual prediction error and whole-bank action ranking do not follow the closed-loop success ordering, whereas CEM-aligned elite regret tracks success more closely. With a frozen V-JEPA 2 encoder and matched DROID post-training, AD-WM also improves zero-shot transfer to our Franka setup, increasing basic pick-and-place success from 42.2% to 71.1% without lab-specific adaptation. These results suggest that world models for planning should preserve action-dependent differences needed for counterfactual selection, rather than optimize factual prediction accuracy alone. More videos and code are available at https://ad-wm.github.io/.
